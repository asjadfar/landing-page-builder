## Copy

- [ ] Headline written from `templates/section-copy-templates.md` and approved by the owner
- [ ] Subheadline written and approved
- [ ] Benefits (3–4) written, each one a fact the owner confirmed
- [ ] Social proof is real only — no invented testimonial, name, rating or number
- [ ] Offer wording matches the owner's real offer (amount, dates, conditions)
- [ ] CTA button text names the action (not "Click here" / "Submit")
- [ ] No claim on the page the owner did not confirm ("licensed", "insured", "guaranteed", "same-day", "best", "number one")
- [ ] No ranking, traffic or conversion promise on the page

## Page structure — all six sections present, in order

- [ ] 1. Hero — headline, subheadline, one clear CTA
- [ ] 2. Benefits — 3–4, outcome-focused
- [ ] 3. Social proof — real only, or honestly left out
- [ ] 4. Offer / CTA — offer restated, same CTA as the hero
- [ ] 5. Contact form — name, email/phone, service needed, message
- [ ] 6. Footer — business name, contact details, service area, policy links (if any), copyright year

## Mobile

- [ ] Single column on mobile (no side-by-side columns below 768 px)
- [ ] No horizontal scroll (tested by scrolling sideways on a phone)
- [ ] Body text at least 16 px, hero headline at least 32 px on mobile
- [ ] Buttons and links at least 44 × 44 px, 8 px apart; CTA button full-width on mobile
- [ ] Hero headline, subheadline and CTA visible on one mobile screen where possible
- [ ] Form fields open the right phone keyboard (email / number); message field at least 4 lines tall
- [ ] Images scale (100% width of container at most, height auto)
- [ ] Checked on a real phone, not only a resized desktop window — phone model / date:

## Speed

- [ ] Every image under 200 KB where possible, none over 500 KB
- [ ] Images resized to the size shown (no 4000 px image shown at 800 px)
- [ ] Alt text on every image
- [ ] No video, map, chat widget or social feed above the fold
- [ ] No extra scripts beyond the builder / form (list any added: ___)
- [ ] One font family, two weights at most
- [ ] Hero appears in about 3 seconds or less on a phone on a mobile connection
