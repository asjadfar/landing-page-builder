# Section Copy Templates — 1-page landing page

> Copy-paste starting points for each section. Fill in every [bracket] with
> the owner's real facts. Never write a claim the owner did not confirm,
> never invent a testimonial, a number, or a price. Delete any template line
> that does not fit the business — do not force all of them in.

## Hero — headline formulas

Pick one formula. Headline under 10 words where possible. The visitor must
understand what the business does, for whom, and what to do next, in 5 seconds.

1. **[Service] in [Area] — [Outcome] in [Time]**
   - Example (fictional): "Home cleaning in Springfield — a spotless home in one visit."
2. **[Outcome] without [Pain]**
   - Example (fictional): "A spotless home, without lifting a finger."
3. **We [do the service] so you can [enjoy the outcome]**
   - Example (fictional): "We clean your home so you can enjoy your weekend."
4. **[Service] for [customer type] in [Area]**
   - Example (fictional): "Deep cleaning for busy families in Springfield."
5. **Your [problem], solved [how fast / how simply]**
   - Example (fictional): "Your messy home, cleaned in one visit."

### Subheadline (one or two sentences)

- What the business does + who it is for + how easy the next step is.
- Example (fictional): "Professional home cleaning in Springfield. Book in 2 minutes — we bring everything we need."
- Example (fictional): "Licensed and insured cleaners, background-checked, with eco-friendly products. Free quotes, no obligation."

### CTA button text

The button must name the action. Never use "Click here" or "Submit".

- "Get My Free Quote"
- "Book My [Service]" — e.g. "Book My Cleaning"
- "Call Now"
- "Message Us on WhatsApp"
- "Get Started"
- "Claim My [Offer]" — e.g. "Claim My 20% Off"

## Benefits — 3–4, outcome-focused

Benefits say what the customer GETS, not what the business HAS. One line each.
Write 3–4. If the owner can only confirm 3, use 3 — do not invent a fourth.

Formula: **[Outcome] — [one short proof or detail]**

Fictional examples (cleaning service — replace with the owner's real benefits):

- "Done in one visit — most homes are fully cleaned in a single 2–3 hour visit."
- "We bring our own supplies — eco-friendly products and equipment included."
- "Weekend slots available — book Saturday or Sunday if weekdays do not work."
- "Free re-clean promise — not happy with a room? We come back and clean it again, free."

Fictional examples (beauty salon — replace with the owner's real benefits):

- "Styled in under an hour — in and out on your lunch break."
- "Products that suit your hair — we match products to your hair type, not the shelf."
- "Evening appointments — open late on weekdays for after-work visits."
- "No-surprise pricing — the price we quote is the price you pay."

Do NOT write: "We are the best", "Number one in [Area]", "Guaranteed results"
— unless the owner confirms the claim in writing and can back it up.

## Social proof — real only

Use ONLY what the owner supplies. If the owner supplies nothing, leave this
section out of the page. Never invent a testimonial, a name, a star rating,
or a number.

### Testimonial format

> "[Customer's exact words, with only spelling fixed and only with the
> owner's approval.]"
> — [First name only], [Area or service used]

- Use first name only unless the customer agreed to a full name.
- Use the customer's exact words. Do not polish them into marketing copy.
- If a customer asked Claude to help word their testimonial, the customer
  must agree with the final text before it goes on the page.

### Numbers format

Write the number exactly as the owner gave it, and note the source.

- "Over [number] homes cleaned in [Area]" — source: owner's records.
- "[Number] years in business" — source: owner.
- "[Rating] stars from [number] reviews on [platform]" — only if the rating
  and count are real and checkable on that platform.

### Logos

- Only logos of real partners, suppliers or memberships the business actually
  has. The owner supplies the logo files and confirms each one may be shown.

### If there is no social proof yet

Do not invent any. Use one of these honest lines instead, or leave the
section out:

- "New customers welcome — first booking discount available."
- "Now taking bookings in [Area]."

## Offer / CTA

Restate the offer plainly: what it is, how much, how long it lasts. Then the
same CTA button as the hero.

Fictional examples:

- "First clean 20% off — book before the end of the month."
- "Free quote, no obligation — tell us what you need and we reply within one business day."
- "Book two rooms, get the third half price — this month only."

Rules:

- The offer must match the owner's real offer exactly (amount, dates, conditions).
- If the offer has an end date, state it. If it has no end date, do not invent one.
- The CTA button here is identical to the hero button and does the same thing.

## Contact form — copy around the form

### Heading

- "Get in touch"
- "Get your free quote"
- "Book your [service]"

### Line above the form (one sentence)

- "Tell us what you need — we reply within one business day."
- "Fill in the form and we will call you back to confirm your booking."

### Confirmation message (shown on screen after submit)

- "Thank you, [Name]. We received your message and will reply within one business day."
- The reply time must be the real one the owner confirms. Never promise
  "within an hour" unless the owner confirms it.
- The confirmation screen also shows the business phone / WhatsApp, so a
  visitor who needs an urgent answer can call instead of waiting.

### Service-needed options

List the owner's real services only, as a dropdown or radio buttons.

- Fictional cleaning example: Regular home clean / Deep clean / Move-in or move-out clean
- Fictional salon example: Haircut / Colour / Styling / Bridal

## Footer

- Business name, service, and area served.
- Email, phone / WhatsApp (exactly as the owner gave them).
- Service area (city / region).
- Links: Privacy Policy and Terms, only if the owner has them.
- Copyright: "© [year] [Business name]. All rights reserved."
- No fake address. If the business has no public address, the footer shows
  contact details only.
